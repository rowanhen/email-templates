# Case Study Email

Social proof at its most powerful — a real customer, a specific result, a compelling story. B2B gold. Works in both nurture and expansion contexts.

## When to Send

- Mid-funnel (prospects who haven't converted after 2–3 weeks)
- To existing customers in the same industry as the featured customer (expansion)
- After a prospect goes dark — re-engagement with proof instead of a pitch
- **Cadence:** 1–2 per quarter per segment; don't flood with case studies

## Key Elements

- **Headline metric** — one specific, credible number ("47% reduction in churn")
- **Customer name + logo** — real attribution builds trust
- **The before state** — what the problem was before your product
- **The after state** — what changed and why
- **Quote** — one sentence from the customer in their own words
- **CTA** — read the full story, or a relevance-based offer ("relevant to you?")

## Best Practices

- Segment by industry, company size, or use case — send the case study closest to the recipient
- Lead with the metric in the subject line
- Short email, link to the full case study — don't paste 800 words into the email
- The customer's name in the subject dramatically increases open rates in B2B

## Common Mistakes

- Vague results ("improved efficiency") — cite specific numbers or don't send it
- Using the same case study for all segments
- No link to the full case study
- Burying the result — it should be in the first two lines

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: How [Company] cut [metric] by [X]%

Hi [Name],

[Company] had a problem you might recognise: [1-sentence description of the problem].

Within [timeframe] of using [your product], they:
- [Result 1 — specific metric]
- [Result 2]

"[Short customer quote]" — [Name], [Title] at [Company]

[CTA: "Read the full story →"]

Worth a look if [relevance hook].
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Open rate** — headline metric in subject drives this
- **CTR to full case study** — measures story engagement
- **Pipeline influence** — deals that touched a case study email
- **Conversion lift** — compare close rates with vs. without case study touchpoint
