# Follow-Up Sequence

A multi-touch cadence of 3–5 emails sent over 2 weeks after an initial cold outreach with no response. Each touchpoint adds new value rather than just bumping the thread.

## When to Send

- After cold outreach email gets no reply within 3–4 business days
- Each follow-up spaced 3–5 days apart
- Stop after 4–5 attempts unless you get a signal (open, click, reply)

## Key Elements

- **New angle each time** — don't just say "just following up"
- **Shorter as the sequence progresses** — email 4 should be 2–3 lines
- **Value-add touchpoints** — share a relevant case study, article, or stat
- **Permission-based exit** — last email makes it easy to say no

## Cadence Structure

| Email | Timing | Angle |
|-------|--------|-------|
| #1 | Day 0 | Cold outreach (see `cold-outreach.md`) |
| #2 | Day 4 | Different angle / new hook |
| #3 | Day 8 | Short value-add (case study, insight) |
| #4 | Day 12 | Bump with humour or bluntness |
| #5 | Day 15 | Breakup email (see `breakup-email.md`) |

## Best Practices

- Reply to the original thread — keeps context, improves deliverability
- Each email should be readable standalone (recipient may not scroll up)
- Change the subject line occasionally to reset open rates
- Mix email with LinkedIn touches between sends

## Common Mistakes

- "Just checking in" with no new content — worthless
- Too many emails too fast — 2 days apart looks desperate
- Giving up after one follow-up — most replies come at email 3–4
- Apologising for following up ("Sorry to bother you again...")

## Typical Structure

**Email 2 — Different angle:**
```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

Hi [Name],

Wanted to try a different angle — [new observation or pain point].

[One-sentence tie to what you do.]

Still think it's relevant for [Company]. Open to a quick chat?

[Name]
```

**Email 3 — Value-add:**
```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

[Name] — thought this might be useful regardless of whether we
ever talk.

[Link or 2-sentence summary of relevant case study/stat/article]

[Company] had a similar situation to yours and got [specific result].

Worth 15 minutes?
```

**Email 4 — Blunt bump:**
```
Subject: Re: [original subject]

[Name] — I've reached out a few times. Either the timing's off,
you're slammed, or this just isn't relevant.

Totally fair if so. Should I close this out?
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Reply rate by email number** — find where your sequence dies
- **Sequence completion rate** — how many prospects make it to email 5
- **Meeting booked rate** — which email in the sequence converts most
