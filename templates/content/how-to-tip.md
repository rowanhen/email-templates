# How-To Tip

One actionable technique per email. The recurring "tip of the week/month" format — high-utility, low-commitment content that builds consistent habit with readers.

## When to Send

- On a fixed schedule (weekly, biweekly)
- As a standalone format or within a larger newsletter
- When you have a genuinely useful, self-contained technique

## Key Elements

- **One tip only** — resist the urge to include extras; the constraint is the format's strength
- **Clear, actionable instruction** — a reader should be able to act on it today
- **Brief context** — why this tip matters, in one or two sentences
- **Optional example** — shows the tip in practice

## Best Practices

- **Test the tip yourself first** — only share techniques you've actually used
- **Keep it short** — the format promises brevity; a 600-word "tip" breaks the contract
- **Name the tip** — give it a short label so it's memorable and searchable
- **Consistent subject line pattern** — readers should know at a glance what this is ("Tip #47:", "This week's trick:")

## Common Mistakes

- Including multiple tips (kills the format)
- Tips that are too vague to act on ("be more organised")
- No example or context — just an instruction without grounding
- Irregular cadence breaks the habit loop

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Tip #{{n}}: {{Short tip name}}

**{{Tip name}}**

{{One sentence: the technique in plain language.}}

{{Why it works — 1–2 sentences.}}

{{Optional: a concrete example showing it in use.}}

Try it this week.

— {{Author name}}
```

## Metrics to Track

- Open rate trend (consistency signals list health)
- Click rate (if tip links to more detail)
- Reply or feedback rate
