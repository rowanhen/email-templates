# Proposal Follow-Up Email

Sent after delivering a proposal or quote to keep momentum without being pushy. The goal is to surface objections early and move toward a decision.

## When to Send

- 2–3 business days after sending the proposal (if no response)
- 24 hours after sending if you have a stated deadline

## Key Elements

- **Acknowledge receipt** — confirm they have everything they need
- **Open the door for questions** — most lost deals die on unasked objections
- **Gentle urgency** — tie timeline to their stated goals, not your quota
- **Easy path to no** — better to get a "no" than silence for weeks

## Best Practices

- Don't attach the proposal again unless they ask — you know they have it
- Reference something specific from your discovery call ("you mentioned Q2 launch...")
- Make it conversational, not a formal nudge email
- If the deal has gone quiet >2 weeks, escalate to a breakup email
- **High-value deals:** if there's been no reply after day 7, call instead — email is low-bandwidth for complex objections

## Common Mistakes

- "Just wanted to check if you had a chance to look at the proposal" — low effort, low return
- Applying pressure before surfacing concerns ("we need an answer by Friday")
- Sending a wall of text restating the proposal
- Following up once and giving up

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Re: [Proposal name] — any questions?

Hi [Name],

Wanted to follow up on the proposal I sent [day/date]. Happy to
walk through any of it if useful.

A couple of things that tend to come up at this stage:
- [Price: "Happy to walk through what's flexible if that's a concern"]
- [Timeline: "If the Q2 date is tight, we can discuss phasing"]

You mentioned [goal from discovery] — does our scoped timeline
still line up with where you're at?

Let me know if you need anything adjusted, or if the timing's
shifted.

[Name]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Response rate** — what % of proposals get a reply within 5 days
- **Win rate** — proposals sent → deals closed
- **Average time-to-close** — from proposal send to signed
