# Referral Program Lifecycle

The full referral journey: invite sent, reward earned, referral converted. Unlike a simple referral ask, this is a structured program with triggers at each stage.

## When to Send

- **Invite email:** 30–60 days post-signup, once the user has seen value
- **Reward earned:** Immediately when a referral signs up or completes a key action
- **Referral converted:** When the referred user converts to paid
- **Re-invite:** 60 days after invite with no referrals (optional, one attempt)

## Key Elements

- **Invite:** Clear mechanics (what you give, what they get), shareable link, social proof
- **Reward earned:** Celebration of the action, what the reward is, when they'll receive it
- **Converted:** Thank the referrer, confirm the reward, encourage another referral

## Best Practices

- Double-sided rewards (both parties benefit) convert 3x better than one-sided
- Make the sharing mechanic obvious — personal link > generic promo code
- Reward immediately on trigger event, not on a manual review cycle
- Keep program mechanics simple — complexity kills participation

## Common Mistakes

- Burying the reward value — lead with it
- Reward that's too small to be worth the social capital of a referral
- No follow-up when a referral signs up (missed engagement moment)
- Long reward delays that erode trust in the program

## Typical Structure

```
-- INVITE EMAIL --
Subject: Give [X], get [Y] — your referral link inside

Hi [Name], you've been using [Product] for [X days] — want to earn [reward]?

Share your link: [unique link]
When [friend] signs up, you both get [reward].

[Share now →]

-- REWARD EARNED --
Subject: You just earned [reward], [Name] 🎉

[Referral name] signed up using your link.
Your [reward] has been added to your account.

You've now referred [N] people. Refer [X] more for [bonus reward].

-- REFERRAL CONVERTED --
Subject: Your referral just upgraded — bonus [reward] incoming

[Name] just became a paying customer.
Your [bonus reward] will hit your account by [date].

Want to keep it going? Your link: [link]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Referral invite open/click rate** — is the program visible enough?
- **Participation rate** — % of users who share their link at least once
- **Referral conversion rate** — referred signups who become paying customers
- **CAC via referral** — compare to paid acquisition cost
- **Virality coefficient (K-factor)** — referrals per active user
