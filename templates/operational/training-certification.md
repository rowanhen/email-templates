# Training & Certification

Learning opportunity email: new courses, skill development programs, certification launches, or training announcements. Motivates action by connecting learning to career or practical outcomes.

## When to Send

- On launch of a new course, certification, or training program
- As a periodic reminder to users who haven't started or completed available training
- When a certification is expiring and renewal is required

## Key Elements

- **What is being offered** — course name, certification, or program type
- **What the learner will gain** — specific skills, credentials, or outcomes
- **Time commitment** — how long it takes; reduces uncertainty and increases starts
- **CTA to enrol or start** — one clear action
- **Deadline or cohort date** if applicable

## Best Practices

- **Lead with the outcome, not the curriculum** — "Get certified in X" is stronger than "Learn about X"
- **Reduce the activation barrier** — state time required upfront; "2 hours" converts better than vague
- **Segment by relevance** — send to users for whom this training is actually applicable
- **Include social proof** — number of completions, reviews, or recognisable companies using the certification

## Common Mistakes

- Listing curriculum topics instead of benefits and outcomes
- No time estimate — learners avoid starting because they can't plan
- Sending to the whole list regardless of relevance
- Missing a clear deadline when one exists (low urgency leads to inaction)

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: New: [Certification or course name] — [Outcome or credential]

**[Course or certification name]**
[One sentence: what you'll be able to do after completing this]

**What's included:**
- [Module or topic]
- [Module or topic]
- [Credential or outcome on completion]

⏱ [Time to complete] | 📅 [Start date or deadline if applicable]

[Optional: "Join [N] others who have already completed this"]

[Enrol now →]
```

## Metrics to Track

- Enrolment rate from email
- Completion rate of enrolled learners
- Certification or credential earned rate
