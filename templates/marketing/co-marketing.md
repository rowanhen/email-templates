# Co-Marketing / Partner Announcement

Joint email campaigns with a partner or integration — both brands, shared audience, mutual value. Used for integration launches, joint offers, or co-authored content.

## When to Send

- On launch day of a new integration or partnership
- Coordinated send with the partner to their list on the same day
- As part of a joint content campaign (shared webinar, guide, report)
- **Avoid:** Announcing partnerships before the product/integration is actually live

## Key Elements

- **Both brands clearly represented** — logo + name for each
- **The joint value prop** — what combining the two products unlocks
- **What's new/available** — the specific integration, offer, or content
- **Audience relevance** — why *this* customer segment cares
- **Single CTA** — one action, not a menu

## Best Practices

- Write for your audience, not the partner's — explain their product briefly
- The value prop must be additive ("together you can now..."), not just "we partnered"
- Coordinate send timing with partner — same-day sends amplify the moment
- Keep both brands visually balanced — avoid one feeling like an ad for the other

## Common Mistakes

- Partnership announcement with no user benefit ("we're excited to announce...")
- Assuming your audience knows the partner's product — explain it in one line
- Two CTAs (one per brand) — kills conversion
- Sending without the partner's list sending simultaneously (missed amplification)

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: [Your Product] + [Partner Product] — [specific joint benefit]

Hi [Name],

[Your Product] and [Partner Product] now work together — which means [joint benefit in one line].

What this unlocks for you:
- [Benefit 1 — specific to the integration]
- [Benefit 2]
- [Benefit 3]

About [Partner Product]: [1-sentence description for context]

[CTA: "Connect [Partner] to [Your Product] →" or "Learn more →"]

[Joint sign-off or both brand logos]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Click-to-integration-setup rate** — primary conversion metric
- **Integration activation rate** — % who complete the connection
- **Cross-list reach** — new contacts acquired from partner's send
- **Revenue influenced** — deals or upgrades attributed to integration usage
