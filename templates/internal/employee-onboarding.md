# Employee Onboarding Email (Day 1)

Welcome email sent to a new hire on their first day. Sets the tone, reduces anxiety, and gets them oriented without overwhelming them.

## When to Send
- Day 1 morning, at or before the start of their working hours
- Send a pre-boarding email 1–3 days before start for logistics (laptop, location, who to ask for)
- Remote hires: this email carries more weight — it may be their primary first impression

## Who Sends It
- **Direct manager:** personal welcome + team context
- **People/HR:** company-wide logistics (systems, policies, first-week schedule)

## Best Practices
- Name the 3–5 things they need to do on day 1, in order — decision fatigue is highest on day 1
- Include the onboarding buddy's name and Slack handle — "ask your manager" is a deterrent; a named buddy is a resource
- For remote hires: explicitly state what "a good first day looks like" — no office cues to follow

## Typical Structure
```
[PRE-BOARDING — send 2–3 days before start:]
Subject: Getting ready for your first day at [Company]

Hi [Name], looking forward to having you on [start date]!
• Laptop: [couriered by [date] / collect at reception]
• Where to go: [location or video link + contact name]
• What to bring: [e.g., ID for right-to-work check / nothing — just show up]
Any questions: [manager@company.com]
[Manager name]

---
[DAY 1:]
Subject: Welcome to [Company], [Name] — your day 1 guide

Hi [Name], welcome! Here's what today looks like:

1. Set up your laptop — [IT setup guide →]
2. Join Slack and say hi in #introductions
3. 10am: intro call with [Manager] — [calendar link]
4. Read the [First Week Guide →] (20 min)
5. 2pm: team standup — [link]

Your go-to this week: [Buddy name] (@handle on Slack)
• [HR portal / benefits — enrol by: X]
• [IT helpdesk: it@company.com]
• [Team handbook →]
[Manager name] · [Title] · [Phone/Slack]
```

## Metrics to Track
- **Day-1 IT/HR support tickets** — high volume = onboarding email missing critical info
- **New hire survey score (30 days)** — "Did you feel prepared on day 1?"
- **Time-to-productivity at 30/60/90 days** — correlates with clarity of early onboarding comms
