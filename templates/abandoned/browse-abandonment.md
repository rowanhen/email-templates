# Browse Abandonment

Recovery email for users who viewed product pages but didn't add anything to cart. Softer intent signal than cart abandonment — the approach should match.

## When to Send

- 2–4 hours after the session ends (not during it)
- Only if they viewed the same product or category multiple times (signals real interest)
- Don't send if they made a purchase in the same session

## Key Elements

- **Show what they viewed** — product name, image, price
- **Low-friction next step** — "take another look" not "buy now"
- **Social proof** — reviews or purchase count for the specific product they viewed
- **No hard sell** — this person hasn't committed; pressure will push them away

## Non-Obvious Patterns

- **Threshold matters** — only trigger after 2+ page views of the same item, or 3+ minutes on a category; casual browsers aren't worth emailing
- **Different intent than cart abandonment** — they're still in consideration phase; your job is to reduce uncertainty, not create urgency
- **"Others are looking at this"** is effective if true and platform-supported — but only use real-time data; stale numbers look fake
- **Answer the likely objection** — if the product has a common hesitation (size, compatibility, returns policy), address it in the email directly
- **One product only** — don't show everything they browsed; pick the most-viewed item and focus on it

## Common Mistakes

- Triggering on a single 5-second product view (too low intent)
- Using the same copy as cart abandonment (wrong tone for the intent level)
- Sending the same day as a cart abandonment email if they browsed then abandoned
- Showing a full catalogue instead of the specific product

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Still thinking about {{product_name}}?

Hi {{name}},

You spent some time looking at {{product_name}} — wanted to make sure you had everything you needed to decide.

[Product image, name, price]

{{Key spec or feature that addresses the common hesitation}}

{{Social proof: "{{n}} people bought this last week" or a short review snippet}}

[View {{product_name}} →]

Any questions? Reply here or visit {{faq_or_chat_link}}.

— {{brand_name}}
```
