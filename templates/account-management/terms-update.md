# Terms of Service / Privacy Policy Update

Notification of material changes to Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, or both.

## When to Send
- Any material change — not typo fixes or minor clarifications
- 30+ days notice minimum; some jurisdictions require more
- Send standalone — never bundle with newsletters or product updates

## Key Elements
- **What's changing** — specific plain English, not "we've updated our terms"
- **Why it's changing** — one sentence rationale builds trust
- **Effective date** — in the subject line and above the fold
- **User impact** — practical consequences, not legal language
- **Both document links** — new version and archived previous version

## Best Practices

- Lead with plain-English "What's changing" — legal text is linked, not pasted
- If change involves data sharing or pricing: offer opt-out before effective date
- Document the send (timestamp, recipient list) — needed to prove legal notice
- Plain text or minimal HTML — this is trust communication, not marketing

## Legal / Compliance Notes

- GDPR: material data-processing changes require re-notification or fresh consent (Art. 6/13)
- If introducing third-party data sharing: explicit opt-in likely required under GDPR
- COPPA: changes affecting under-13s require parental notification
- CCPA: material privacy changes require notice at or before point of collection
- GDPR: passive acceptance ("continued use = acceptance") may be insufficient for material data-processing changes — seek legal review before relying on it

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Changes to [Product] [Terms / Privacy Policy] — effective [date]

Hi [Name],

We're updating our [Terms of Service / Privacy Policy], effective [date].

What's changing:
• [Change 1 in plain English]
• [Change 2 in plain English]

Why: [One sentence rationale]

[Read the updated Terms →]    [Compare to previous version →]

Continued use after [date] = acceptance of updated terms.
To close your account before then: [close account link]

Questions? [legal@company.com]
[Legal entity name] · [Address]
```

## Metrics to Track
- **Open rate** — benchmark: 30-50%; lower signals deliverability or subject line issues
- **Account closures within 30 days** — spike = change more disruptive than expected
- **Support/legal queries** — high volume = plain-English summary wasn't clear enough
