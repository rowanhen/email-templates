# Nurture Sequence (Educational Drip)

Multi-touch sequence for leads interested but not yet ready to buy. Build trust through useful content — not sales pitch on every email.

## When to Use
- Lead magnet download, free-trial no-convert, webinar registrant who didn't book a call
- Typical cadence: 5–8 emails over 4–6 weeks
- Exit the sequence immediately if lead books a demo, starts a trial, or replies

## Sequence Structure

| Email | Timing | Goal |
|-------|--------|------|
| 1 | Immediately | Deliver lead magnet / confirm interest |
| 2 | Day 3 | Teach one core concept relevant to their pain |
| 3 | Day 7 | Case study — real outcome, not feature list |
| 4 | Day 14 | Address the most common objection |
| 5 | Day 21 | Another concept; soft CTA |
| 6 | Day 28 | Direct ask — demo, trial, or call |

## Best Practices
- Each email completable in under 2 minutes — link out for longer content
- Teach something genuinely useful every email; don't hold value hostage to conversion
- Vary format across the sequence: how-to, case study, myth-bust, comparison — same angle 6× kills engagement
- Name the sequence in your ESP so sales can see which stage a lead is in
- If no engagement by email 6, move to re-engagement drip or remove entirely

## Typical Structure

Vary opener style across the sequence — using pain-agitation every email is formula-recognisable and trains readers to skim. Rotate through: leading with a counter-intuitive insight, opening with a short story or example, asking a genuine question, or stating a surprising fact.

```
[Email 1 — deliver value immediately:]
Subject: Your [lead magnet] + one thing most people miss

Hi [Name], here's [what they signed up for →].
One thing to keep in mind as you read it: [brief insight].
Next week: [tease topic]
[Name], [Company] | Unsubscribe: [link]

---
[Email 2 — insight-led opener:]
Subject: The real reason [problem] keeps happening (not what you think)

Hi [Name],

Here's something counterintuitive: [surprising insight — lead with the idea, not the pain].

[1–2 sentence explanation]

The practical takeaway: [actionable tip]

Next week: [tease topic]
[Name], [Company] | Unsubscribe: [link]
```

## Metrics to Track
- **Sequence completion rate** — % who reach email 6 without unsubscribing or converting
- **Conversion rate by email** — which email triggers the most demo/trial requests
- **Unsubscribe rate per email** — spike on a specific email = content or timing misaligned
