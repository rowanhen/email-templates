# Webinar Invitation Sequence

The full webinar lifecycle: initial invite, 24h reminder, 1h reminder, and post-event follow-up with replay. Different from event-promotion drip — this is the operational sequence for a single event.

## When to Send

- **Invite:** 7–14 days before the event
- **Reminder (24h):** Day before — re-engagement and logistics
- **Reminder (1h):** 60 minutes before — link prominently, no fluff
- **Follow-up:** Within 24h post-event — replay link, CTA, next step
- **Suppress follow-up** for no-shows who didn't engage (or send a separate no-show variant)

## Key Elements

- **Invite:** Topic, speaker, date/time (with timezone), registration CTA
- **24h reminder:** Logistics recap, "what you'll learn", calendar link
- **1h reminder:** Join link front and centre — nothing else matters
- **Follow-up:** Thank attendees, replay link, CTA tied to webinar topic

## Best Practices

- Include a "add to calendar" link in the invite and 24h reminder
- Speaker credibility boosts registration rates — name and title in the subject
- 1h reminder should be under 100 words — just the link and the time
- Post-event CTA should be directly tied to the webinar topic (not generic)
- Segment attendees vs. no-shows in post-event follow-up

## Common Mistakes

- No 1h reminder — biggest drop-off prevention email, often skipped
- Post-event email that arrives 3+ days later
- Registration form with too many fields (name + email only to start)
- No replay for attendees who had to leave early

## Typical Structure

```
-- INVITE --
Subject: [Webinar Title] — [Date], [Time] [TZ] | Free

Join [Speaker Name] ([Title]) for a [X-min] live session on [topic].

You'll learn: [3 bullet takeaways]
[Date] · [Time] · [Duration] · Free

[CTA: "Save my spot →"]

-- 24H REMINDER --
Subject: Tomorrow: [Webinar Title] — your link inside

[Name], your webinar is tomorrow at [Time] [TZ].

[Join link] · [Add to calendar]

Come ready to discuss: [1-line topic hook]

-- 1H REMINDER --
Subject: Starting in 1 hour — join link inside

[Webinar Title] starts at [time].
Join here: [link]

See you soon.

-- POST-EVENT FOLLOW-UP --
Subject: The replay is ready, [Name]

Thanks for joining [Webinar Title].

Watch the replay: [link]
Download the slides: [link]

Next step: [CTA tied to webinar topic — demo, trial, guide]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Registration rate** — invite clicks to signups
- **Show rate** — registrants who actually attend (industry avg: 35–45%)
- **Replay views** — post-event engagement from non-attendees
- **Post-event CTA conversion** — the real ROI metric
- **Drop-off by email** — which reminder has lowest impact on attendance?
