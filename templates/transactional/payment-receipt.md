# Payment Receipt / Invoice

Confirms that money has been charged. For SaaS, this is often the monthly/annual renewal receipt.

## When to Send

- Immediately after successful payment
- On subscription renewal

## Key Elements

- **Amount charged** — clear, unambiguous
- **Payment method** — last 4 digits of card, PayPal, etc.
- **Date of charge**
- **Invoice number** — for accounting/expense reports
- **Line items** — what the charge covers
- **PDF attachment** — downloadable invoice (many businesses need this)
- **Billing portal link** — to update payment method or view history

## Best Practices

- Include a PDF invoice attachment — finance teams need these
- Subject should include the amount: "Receipt: £49.00 for [Product]"
- Clearly state the next billing date for subscriptions
- Make it trivially easy to update payment method
- Comply with local invoicing requirements (VAT number, company address, etc.)

## Common Mistakes

- No downloadable invoice (forces users to screenshot the email)
- Missing VAT/tax breakdown (causes issues for B2B customers)
- Not stating when the next charge will be
- Hard to find billing management link
- Confusing charge descriptions ("PROD_TIER2_MONTHLY" instead of "Pro Plan — Monthly")
