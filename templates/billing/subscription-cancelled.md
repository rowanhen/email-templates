# Subscription Cancelled

Confirmation sent immediately when a subscription is cancelled — by user, after dunning, or by admin.

## When to Send

- Immediately on cancellation, regardless of trigger
- Distinguish "cancelled at period end" vs. "cancelled immediately" in the copy

## Key Elements

- **Confirmation** — clear statement that cancellation is done
- **Access window** — exact date and time access ends (not "soon")
- **Data handling** — what happens to data and when it's deleted
- **Export link** — always include, regardless of cancellation reason
- **Re-subscribe path** — low-friction, no guilt, just available

## Best Practices

- No guilt-trips or dark patterns ("Are you SURE?") in this email — it's too late
- Always include an export / data download link even if you delete nothing
- Keep a winback door open — a simple "come back anytime" line, not a plea
- Distinguish period-end cancellation from immediate in the body copy

## Legal / Compliance Notes

- EU/UK: confirm in writing that recurring charges will stop (required)
- FTC / CMA: cancellation confirmation helps prove cancellation was as easy as signup
- Retain cancellation records for billing dispute resolution
- If collecting payment data: note it will be deleted per your privacy policy

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Your [Product] subscription has been cancelled

Hi [Name],

Your [Plan Name] subscription has been cancelled.

Access until:  [end of billing period date]
Data deleted:  [date, typically 30 days later]

Download your data before then: [export link]

Changed your mind? Reactivate anytime: [reactivation link]

One quick question — why did you leave? (10 seconds): [survey link]

It's been a pleasure having you — thank you for being part of [Company].
[Name], [Company]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Reactivation within 30/90 days** — benchmark: 5-15% for self-serve cancellations
- **Exit survey response rate** — benchmark: 10-20% click-through
- **Chargeback rate post-cancellation** — should be near zero with a clear confirmation
