# Privacy Policy Update

Notification of material changes to the Privacy Policy — data collection, retention, sharing, or processing purposes.

> For Terms of Service changes, see [account-management/terms-update.md](../account-management/terms-update.md). Privacy Policy changes carry higher legal weight under GDPR: changes affecting the *legal basis* for processing may require fresh opt-in, not just notice.

## When to Send
- Any change to: what data you collect, retention periods, who you share with, or legal basis for processing
- 30+ days notice before changes take effect — seek legal review if adding new data-sharing arrangements
- Standalone email — never bundle with newsletters or product updates

## Legal / Compliance Notes
- **GDPR Art. 13/14:** If processing purpose changes, notify data subjects *before* the new purpose applies
- **GDPR Art. 7:** Changes affecting the basis of consent may invalidate prior consent — re-consent may be required
- **CCPA:** Material privacy changes require notice before collection — prior customers need proactive email
- **COPPA:** Any change affecting under-13 data requires fresh verifiable parental consent
- Document send timestamp, recipient list, and acknowledged-by records — required if a regulator requests evidence

## Best Practices
- Lead with plain-English "What's changing" — link to the full policy, don't paste legal text
- If introducing new data sharing: offer opt-out *before* the effective date
- Provide a diff or changelog link — builds trust and reduces support load
- Plain text or minimal HTML — this is a legal notice, not a marketing email

## Typical Structure
```
Subject: Update to [Company] Privacy Policy — effective [date]

Hi [Name],

We're updating our Privacy Policy, effective [date].

What's changing:
• [Change 1 in plain English — e.g., "We're adding [Vendor] as a data processor"]
• [Change 2 — e.g., "Extending data retention from 12 to 24 months"]

Why: [One sentence rationale]

[Read the updated Privacy Policy →]
[Compare to previous version →]

To opt out before [date]: [delete account] or [privacy@company.com]

[Legal entity] · [Address] · [DPO contact if applicable]
```

## Metrics to Track
- **Open rate** — benchmark 30–50%; lower signals deliverability issues
- **Account deletion rate within 30 days** — spike = change more impactful than anticipated; seek legal review
- **Privacy@ query volume** — high volume = plain-English summary wasn't clear enough
