# Webinar Invitation Sequence

Full webinar lifecycle: invite, reminders, and post-event follow-up with replay. Different from event-promotion drip — this is the operational sequence for a single event.

## When to Send

- **Invite:** 7–14 days before the event
- **24h reminder:** Day before — logistics and re-engagement
- **1h reminder:** 60 minutes before — join link only, no fluff
- **Follow-up:** Within 24h post-event — replay + CTA

## Key Elements

- **Invite:** Topic, speaker (name + title), date/time with timezone, registration CTA
- **24h reminder:** Logistics recap, calendar link
- **1h reminder:** Join link front and centre — nothing else
- **Follow-up:** Replay link, slides, topic-relevant CTA

## Best Practices

- Speaker credibility in the subject line boosts registration rates
- 1h reminder should be under 100 words — just the link and the time
- Segment attendees vs. no-shows in post-event follow-up
- Registration form: name + email only to start — reduce friction

## Common Mistakes

- No 1h reminder — biggest drop-off prevention email, often skipped
- Post-event email that arrives 3+ days later (momentum lost)
- Registration form with too many fields
- No replay for attendees who left early

## Typical Structure

```
-- INVITE --
Subject: [Speaker] on [Topic] — [Date], [Time] [TZ] | Free

Join [Speaker] ([Title]) for [X min] on [topic].
You'll learn: [3 bullet takeaways]

[CTA: "Save my spot →"]

-- 1H REMINDER --
Subject: Starting in 1 hour — join link inside

[Webinar Title] starts at [time]. Join here: [link]

-- POST-EVENT --
Subject: The replay is ready, [Name]

Replay: [link] · Slides: [link]
Next step: [CTA tied to webinar topic]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Registration rate** — invite clicks to signups
- **Show rate** — registrants who attend (benchmark: 35–45%)
- **Replay views** — post-event engagement
- **Post-event CTA conversion** — the real ROI metric
