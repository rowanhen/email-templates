# Newsletter

Regular cadence email (weekly, biweekly, monthly) that delivers value and keeps your brand top-of-mind.

## When to Send

- Consistent schedule — same day and time each week/month
- Tuesday-Thursday mornings tend to perform best (but test for your audience)

## Key Elements

- **Compelling subject line** — the single biggest driver of open rates
- **Curated content** — don't just list everything, pick the best 3-5 items
- **Brief editorial voice** — a short intro that feels human
- **Clear sections** — scannable layout with headers
- **One primary CTA** — even in a newsletter, guide attention
- **Unsubscribe link** — legally required, and hiding it hurts trust

## Best Practices

- Deliver value first, promote second — the 80/20 rule
- Keep a consistent format so readers know what to expect
- Personalise where possible (name, interests, past behaviour)
- Test subject lines — A/B test with a portion, send winner to the rest
- Preview text matters as much as the subject line
- Segment your list — not everyone wants the same content

## Common Mistakes

- Inconsistent schedule — skipping weeks then sending three in a row
- Too long — newsletter fatigue is real
- All promotion, no value — reads like an ad, not a newsletter
- No clear hierarchy — everything looks equally important
- Sending to the entire list without segmentation
- Ignoring preview text (the snippet shown after the subject line)

## Metrics to Track

- **Open rate** — benchmark: 20-30% (varies by industry)
- **Click-through rate** — benchmark: 2-5%
- **Unsubscribe rate** — should be under 0.5% per send
- **List growth rate** — net new subscribers minus unsubscribes
