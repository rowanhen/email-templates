# Referral Request

Asking happy customers to refer someone they know. The timing and framing matter more than the incentive.

## When to Send

- After a strong CSAT score or positive review
- After a customer hits a meaningful milestone in your product
- After a renewal or upgrade (high-satisfaction signal)
- Never cold — only send when you have evidence they're happy

## Key Elements

- **Acknowledge the relationship first** — don't open with "refer a friend"
- **Be specific about who to refer** — "someone running a small ecommerce store" beats "anyone who might be interested"
- **Make the ask easy** — a forwarded email template or a personal referral link, not a signup form
- **Incentive (if any)** — lead with their reward, not yours

## Best Practices

- **Ask for one person, not "anyone you know"** — specificity lowers cognitive load and increases follow-through
- **Give them the words** — include a pre-written blurb they can copy-paste or forward verbatim
- **Two-sided incentives convert 2–3× better** than one-sided ones (both parties benefit)
- **Don't expire the referral link too soon** — referrals happen organically, not on your schedule

## Common Mistakes

- Asking too early before value has been established
- Making them navigate a referral portal instead of just forwarding an email
- **Not disclosing incentives** — FTC guidelines require clear disclosure when a reward is involved; include a disclosure in any forwarded blurb (e.g. "Full disclosure: I get [X] if you sign up")
- Following up multiple times on the same referral ask

## Metrics to Track

- Referral link click-through rate
- Referee conversion rate (signed up / clicked)
- Revenue attributed to referred customers

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Know someone who'd get value from {{product}}?

Hi {{name}},

Really glad {{product}} has been working well for you — {{specific outcome or milestone}}.

If you know anyone dealing with {{specific problem}}, I'd love an introduction.

Here's a blurb you can forward:
---
"I've been using {{product}} for {{timeframe}} and it's helped us {{outcome}}.
Worth a look: {{referral_link}} (Disclosure: I'll receive {{incentive}} if you sign up.)"
---

If they sign up, you'll both get {{incentive}}.

— {{sender_name}}
```
