# Cold Outreach Email

First-touch cold email to a prospect who has never heard from you. Uses the AIDA framework to earn attention and prompt a reply — not a sale.

## When to Send

- First contact with a new prospect
- After researching them enough to personalize meaningfully (company, role, recent news)

## Key Elements

- **Attention** — subject line or opening that earns the click
- **Interest** — one specific observation about them or their company
- **Desire** — concise value prop tied to a pain point they actually have
- **Action** — single low-friction ask (reply, not "book a 45-min demo")

## Best Practices

- Keep it under 100 words — shorter wins in cold outreach
- Lead with *them*, not with you ("I noticed you just hired 3 AEs" > "We help sales teams...")
- One ask only — reply, not "click here, book here, or call us"
- Send Tuesday–Thursday, 7–9 AM in their timezone
- Plain text performs better than HTML (looks personal, avoids spam filters)

## Common Mistakes

- Opening with "My name is X and I work at Y" — nobody cares yet
- Asking for a meeting in the first email — too much too soon
- Generic personalization ("I love what you're doing at [Company]")
- Using tracked HTML templates — they look like mass email and trigger spam filters
- Long paragraphs explaining your entire product

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: [specific hook — question, observation, or mutual connection]

Hi [First Name],

[One sentence specific to them — a LinkedIn post, a recent hire,
a job listing, a competitor move, a public metric.]

[One sentence connecting that observation to a pain you solve.]

[One sentence on what you do, framed as an outcome they'd want.]

Worth a quick reply if this is on your radar?

[First Name]
[Title] at [Company]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Open rate** — benchmark: 30-50% (cold); subject line lever
- **Reply rate** — benchmark: 2-8%; the real metric that matters
- **Positive reply rate** — strip out unsubscribes from reply rate
