# Milestone Celebration Email

Celebrating a user's achievement or anniversary within your product. Done well, these build emotional connection and product attachment. Done badly, they're hollow.

## When to Send

- User hits a meaningful in-product achievement (100th X, first Y, completed Z)
- Account anniversary (1 year, 2 years with you)
- Team/company milestone (100th user on a team account)
- Product-defined progress markers (completed onboarding, published first X)

## Key Elements

- **Specific achievement** — name the exact thing they did, not a generic "you're doing great"
- **Real number or outcome** — "You've processed 500 invoices" beats "You've been busy!"
- **Light CTA or no CTA** — this is a celebration, not a conversion email
- **Human warmth** — can be playful or heartfelt, depending on your brand voice

## Best Practices

- The milestone should be genuinely meaningful to the user, not to your metrics
- Use their actual data — personalised stats make these emails worth opening
- Don't overcelebrate trivial actions — it feels patronising
- Annual anniversaries are a good moment to invite a referral or review, subtly
- Keep it short — the achievement is the point, not the email

## Common Mistakes

- Celebrating a milestone that the user doesn't actually care about
- Turning a celebration into a sales email ("Congrats! Upgrade to get more...")
- Sending too many milestone emails — devalues each one
- Generic copy that could apply to any user
- Missing the emotional beat — flat, corporate tone on something meant to feel warm

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: 🎉 You just hit [milestone] — here's what that means

Hi [Name],

You just [specific achievement] — and that's worth noting.

[One stat or fact that puts the achievement in context:
"That's more than 90% of [Product] users in your first year."]

[Optional: light reflection on what they've built/done]

[Optional CTA — only if it serves them: "See your full stats →"
or "Share this milestone" or nothing at all]

Thanks for being here.

[Name/Team]
```

**Example — SaaS anniversary:**
```
Subject: One year of [Product] 🎂

Hi Jamie,

A year ago today you sent your first campaign through [Product].

Since then: 47 campaigns, 128,000 emails sent, 34% average open
rate (industry average: 21%).

Not bad.

Thanks for sticking with us — and for the trust that comes with
that. Here's to year two.

— The [Product] team
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Open rate** — milestone emails typically hit 50%+ when genuinely relevant
- **Click rate** — if there's a CTA
- **Churn rate correlation** — users who receive milestone emails vs. those who don't
