# Welcome Email

The first email a user receives after signing up. Sets the tone for the entire relationship.

## When to Send

- Immediately after account creation / signup
- Should arrive within seconds, not minutes

## Key Elements

- **Warm greeting** — use their name if available
- **Value reminder** — restate what they signed up for and why it matters
- **Single clear CTA** — one action to take next (not five)
- **What to expect** — brief mention of what emails they'll receive
- **Support access** — how to get help if needed

## Best Practices

- Keep it short — under 150 words in the body
- One primary CTA button, not multiple competing links
- Send from a real person's name (not "noreply@")
- Include an unsubscribe link (legally required in most jurisdictions)
- Mobile-first design — most users open on mobile

## Common Mistakes

- Too much information — overwhelming the user on day one
- No clear next step — the user reads it and thinks "now what?"
- Generic tone — feels like every other SaaS welcome email
- Delayed delivery — arriving hours later kills momentum
- Multiple CTAs competing for attention

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Welcome to [Product] — here's your first step

Hi [Name],

Welcome aboard! You signed up for [Product] to [value prop].

Here's what to do next:

[Primary CTA Button: "Complete Your Setup" / "Start Your First X"]

If you need anything, just reply to this email.

[Sign-off from a real person]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Open rate** — benchmark: 50-60% (welcome emails get high opens)
- **Click-through rate** — benchmark: 20-30%
- **Time to first action** — did they complete the CTA?
