# Onboarding Sequence

A series of emails (typically 3-7) sent over the first 1-2 weeks to guide new users toward activation.

## When to Send

- Drip over first 7-14 days after signup
- Timing depends on product complexity and activation milestones

## Typical Sequence

| Day | Email | Goal |
|-----|-------|------|
| 0 | Welcome | Set expectations, first CTA |
| 1 | Quick win | Guide to first value moment |
| 3 | Feature highlight | Show a key feature they haven't tried |
| 5 | Social proof | Case study or testimonial |
| 7 | Check-in | Ask if they need help |
| 10 | Upgrade nudge | (if freemium) Show premium value |
| 14 | Re-engage or celebrate | Based on whether they're active |

## Best Practices

- **Behaviour-triggered, not just time-based** — skip emails for features they've already used
- Each email should have ONE focus and ONE CTA
- Progressively reveal complexity — don't dump everything at once
- Make it easy to reply and get human help
- Include progress indicators ("You've completed 2 of 4 setup steps")

## Common Mistakes

- Sending the same sequence to everyone regardless of behaviour
- Too many emails too fast — respect inbox space
- All emails are feature tours — mix in social proof and quick wins
- No exit conditions — keep emailing even after they've activated
- Inconsistent tone/design across the sequence

## Metrics to Track

- **Activation rate** — % who complete key actions during onboarding
- **Sequence completion rate** — how many open/click through the whole series
- **Unsubscribe rate per email** — identifies which emails are too pushy
- **Time to activation** — is the sequence accelerating this?
