# Offboarding Emails

Two related emails for employee departures: one to the departing employee, one to the team.

## When to Send
- **To the leaver:** Day 1 of notice period, or same day as departure decision if sudden
- **To the team:** Same day or day before the leaver's last day — *after* the leaver has been told (never announce to the team before the person knows)

## Legal / Compliance Notes
- **Data access:** Revoke system access on final day (or immediately if involuntary); document the timestamp
- **GDPR:** Inform the leaver what employee data you retain and for how long per your HR data retention policy
- **References:** Do not include anything in the team email characterising the leaver's performance — creates legal exposure

## Best Practices (to the leaver)
- Confirm last day, final payroll date, and equity vesting status in writing — reduces ambiguity and anxiety
- Explain equipment return, reference process, and post-departure payslip access clearly
- Keep tone professional and warm — how you treat leavers shapes how current employees feel about the company

## Best Practices (to the team)
- One sentence on contribution; one sentence on next steps for their work — that's the whole email
- If their role is being filled, say so (or say you'll share more soon)
- Do not share reasons for departure unless the leaver has agreed to the framing

## Typical Structure
```
[TO THE LEAVER:]
Subject: Your departure from [Company] — next steps

Hi [Name],

Your last day is [date]. Here's what to expect:
• Final payroll: [date]
• Equipment return: [instructions / contact]
• System access: deactivated on [date]
• Reference requests: [HR/manager email]
• Payslip access post-departure: [portal link, available until X]

Exit interview (optional): [link or invite]

Thank you for [X] at [Company]. We wish you well.
[Manager or HR name]

---
[TO THE TEAM:]
Subject: [Name] is leaving [Company]

Hi [Team],

[Name] will be leaving [Company]; their last day is [date].
[One sentence on their contribution.]
[Their work] will be covered by [person/plan] — more detail to follow.

Please join me in wishing [Name] well.
[Manager name]
```

## Metrics to Track
- **Exit interview completion rate** — higher when the leaver email includes a clear, low-friction invite
- **Equipment return compliance** — % returned on time; directly correlates with clarity of the leaver email
- **Glassdoor/employer review sentiment** — offboarding quality is a top predictor of post-departure reviews
