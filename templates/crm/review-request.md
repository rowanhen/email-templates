# Review Request

Asking customers to leave a public review on G2, Trustpilot, Google, or similar. The window is narrow and the ask needs to be friction-free.

## When to Send

- Within 24 hours of a positive CSAT response
- After a renewal, upgrade, or stated satisfaction signal
- Never to customers with open support tickets or recent complaints

## Key Elements

- **Pick one platform** — don't ask for three reviews in one email
- **Link directly to the review form**, not the product profile page
- **Time estimate** — "takes 2 minutes" sets the expectation
- **Why it matters** — a brief, honest reason (helps other buyers decide, helps the team)

## Non-Obvious Patterns

- **Pre-qualify with a soft question before the ask**: "On a scale of 1–5, how likely are you to recommend us?" — route 4s and 5s to the review link, route 1–3 to a feedback form
- **Choose platform by customer segment**: B2B SaaS → G2 or Capterra; local/service → Google; consumer → Trustpilot
- **Include a suggested structure** — customers freeze at blank review boxes; prompt with: "What was your situation before? What changed?" This also produces better reviews
- **Send from a person** — a review request from "Sarah" gets more responses than from "The [Company] Team"
- **Midweek morning** (Tuesday–Thursday, 9–11am) consistently outperforms for review requests

## Common Mistakes

- Sending the same email to all customers regardless of satisfaction
- Linking to the main profile page (adds steps; kills conversion)
- Incentivising reviews on platforms that prohibit it (violates ToS)
- Sending multiple review requests — once is enough

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Would you mind sharing your experience?

Hi {{name}},

I'm so glad {{outcome_or_positive_signal}}.

If you have 2 minutes, an honest review would mean a lot — it helps other {{customer_type}} decide if {{product}} is right for them.

Leave a review: {{direct_review_link}}

Not sure what to write? Try:
- What were you trying to solve before {{product}}?
- What's changed since?

Totally optional — but genuinely appreciated.

— {{name}}
```
