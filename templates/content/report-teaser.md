# Report Teaser

Key stats and findings from a report or resource, with a CTA to download the full version. A lead magnet delivery or gated-content promotion pattern.

## When to Send

- On release of a new report, guide, or research piece
- As a nurture email for leads who fit the report's topic
- When you want to drive downloads or list sign-ups via gated content

## Key Elements

- **2–4 headline stats or findings** — the most striking data points; enough to create interest without giving everything away
- **Clear framing of what the full report covers** — readers need to know what they'd get
- **Single, prominent CTA** — one button or link to download/access
- **Credibility signal** — sample size, methodology note, or source

## Best Practices

- **Lead with the most surprising finding** — not the most obvious
- **Don't give it all away** — tease enough to create desire; the email is a trailer, not a summary
- **Keep the design simple** — stat-heavy emails can clutter; let the numbers breathe
- **Match the CTA to the gate** — "Download the report" is clearer than "Learn more"

## Common Mistakes

- Sharing so much data that the download feels unnecessary
- Burying the CTA below dense copy
- Using generic stats that don't surprise or intrigue
- No framing — just dumping numbers without context

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: [Surprising stat from the report] — new [report/guide/research]

[1–2 sentences framing the report topic and why it matters now]

**What we found:**
- [Stat or finding #1]
- [Stat or finding #2]
- [Stat or finding #3]

[One sentence on what else is inside the full report]

[Download the full report →]

[Credibility note: "Based on X respondents / sources"]
```

## Metrics to Track

- Click-to-download rate
- Conversion rate (email → gated form completion)
- Downstream engagement from downloaders
