# Seasonal Campaign

Holiday and promotional campaigns tied to calendar moments — Black Friday, end-of-year, New Year, Q1 planning. Urgency and discount psychology are the mechanics; relevance and timing are the craft.

## When to Send

- **Black Friday/Cyber Monday:** Teaser (Mon), launch (Thurs/Fri), extension (Mon)
- **End-of-year:** Last week of December — "use it or lose it" budget hooks
- **New Year:** First 2 weeks of January — planning, goals, fresh-start framing
- **General rule:** Prepare creative 4 weeks out; segment 2 weeks out; send on time

## Key Elements

- **Urgency** — deadline that's real (not fake countdown timers)
- **Clear offer** — exact discount, what it applies to, how to redeem
- **Seasonal hook** — tie the campaign to a real reason, not just "sale"
- **Expiry** — hard deadline prominently displayed

## Best Practices

- Start earlier than you think — inboxes are crowded on peak days
- Tiered offers (spend more, save more) increase AOV
- Re-send to non-openers with a different subject line 24h later
- Segment by customer tier — loyal customers deserve exclusive early access
- End-of-year campaigns work well with "you've achieved X this year" data

## Common Mistakes

- Generic "holiday sale!" with no specific offer or deadline
- Fake urgency (countdown that resets) — destroys trust permanently
- Too many emails in the sequence — 3 max per campaign window
- No mobile optimisation — 60%+ of seasonal opens are on mobile
- Ignoring pricing display rules — EU Omnibus Directive requires showing the prior 30-day lowest price alongside discounts; UK Consumer Protection from Unfair Trading Regulations have similar requirements

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: [X]% off — ends [date] 🎁

Hi [Name],

For [holiday/reason], we're offering [specific discount] on [product/plan].

Use code [CODE] at checkout — expires [date/time].

What you get:
- [Offer detail 1]
- [Offer detail 2]

[CTA: "Claim your [X]% off →"]

Offer valid through [hard deadline]. No extensions.
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Revenue per email sent** — the core metric for promotional campaigns
- **Redemption rate** — % who use the offer code
- **AOV during campaign** — are customers spending more or less per transaction?
- **Unsubscribe spike** — monitor closely; seasonal campaigns have higher churn risk
- **List fatigue** — open rate trend across the campaign sequence
