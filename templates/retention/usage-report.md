# Usage Report Email

Periodic summary of how a user or team has been using the product — their activity, outcomes, and value delivered. Think Spotify Wrapped for SaaS: make users feel the product is working for them.

## When to Send

- Monthly or quarterly, on a fixed schedule
- After a significant period of activity (end of a project, end of trial)
- Annual "Year in Review" version for high impact and shareability

## Key Elements

- **Personal stats** — real numbers specific to this user, not averages
- **Outcomes over activity** — "You saved X hours" beats "You ran X reports"
- **Comparison context** — vs. their own previous period, or vs. peers/benchmarks
- **Forward-looking hook** — one thing they could do next to get more value

## Best Practices

- Lead with the most impressive stat — first sentence sets the tone
- Use visual hierarchy even in plain-text emails (line breaks, short bullets)
- If you have a "vs. last month" comparison, always include it
- Quarterly > monthly for most SaaS — monthly can feel like noise
- Annual reports warrant more investment: HTML layout, shareable badge

## Common Mistakes

- Reporting vanity metrics that don't reflect real value ("You logged in 22 times")
- Showing stats that reveal low usage — know your thresholds and suppress for inactive users
- No narrative — raw numbers without context land flat
- Making it look like a marketing email instead of "your data"
- Forgetting mobile: stat-heavy emails need scannable layout

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Your [Month/Quarter] with [Product]

Hi [Name],

Here's what [Product] did for you this [period]:

📊 [Headline stat — the most impressive outcome number]
⏱ [Time saved or efficiency metric]
📈 [Trend vs. previous period: "up X% from last quarter"]

[1-2 sentence narrative: "Your team's busiest week was [date].
You processed X in a single day — a new record for your account."]

Top this period:
• [Activity or outcome 1]
• [Activity or outcome 2]
• [Activity or outcome 3]

One thing worth trying next: [specific feature or action that
would add value based on their usage pattern]

[CTA: "See your full report →" or "Explore [Feature] →"]

[Name/Team]
```

**Annual variant subject lines:**
- "Your 2025 in [Product] 🎯"
- "What [Product] did for you this year"
- "[Name], your year by the numbers"

## Metrics to Track

- **Open rate** — benchmark: 40–60% (high for transactional-feeling sends)
- **Feature discovery CTR** — users who click through to try the recommended feature
- **Correlation with retention** — do users who engage with reports churn less?
