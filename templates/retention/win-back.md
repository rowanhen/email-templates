# Win-Back Email

Re-engaging a user who has already churned — cancelled, lapsed, or gone dark for 30–90+ days. Harder than churn prevention; works best with a genuine reason to come back.

## When to Send

- 30 days post-cancellation (first attempt)
- 60–90 days post-cancellation (second attempt if no response)
- After a significant product update that addresses why they likely left
- Avoid: immediately after cancellation (too raw) or >6 months (likely irrelevant)

## Key Elements

- **Acknowledge the gap** — don't pretend they never left
- **What's changed** — give them a real reason to try again (new feature, pricing, improvement)
- **Low barrier re-entry** — extended trial, one-click restore, no credit card needed
- **Exit reason reference** — if you collected cancellation reasons, use them

## Best Practices

- Segment by cancellation reason if you have it (price, missing feature, didn't use it, competitor)
- "New feature" win-backs outperform generic "we miss you" campaigns significantly
- Keep it honest — "we've been working on X since you left" is more compelling than hype
- A/B test incentive vs. no incentive; sometimes the product update alone is enough
- Limit to 2 attempts — after that, remove from win-back sequences

## Common Mistakes

- Immediate post-cancel win-back (within 24–48h) — looks desperate
- No clear reason why now is different from when they left
- Vague "we've made improvements" without specifics
- Long email that feels like a marketing newsletter
- Not honouring their unsubscribe if they opted out of marketing

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: A lot has changed since you left, [Name]

Hi [Name],

It's been [X months] since you cancelled [Product]. We've been
heads-down since then, and I wanted to share what's new — in
case any of it changes things.

Since [month/year] we've shipped:
- [Feature 1 — tie to a pain if you know it]
- [Feature 2]
- [Pricing/plan change if relevant]

If [original reason for leaving] was the issue, it's worth
another look.

[CTA: "Reactivate with 30 days free" or "See what's new →"]

Either way, no pressure — just wanted you to know.

[Name]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Win-back rate** — churned users who reactivate within 30 days of email
- **Reactivation retention** — do win-backs stick, or churn again within 90 days?
- **Revenue recovered** — ARR/MRR recovered from win-back campaigns
