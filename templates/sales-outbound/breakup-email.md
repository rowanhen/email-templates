# Breakup Email

The final email in an outbound sequence. Creates urgency by walking away — you're closing the loop. Counterintuitively, these often get the highest reply rates in a sequence.

## When to Send

- Final email in a cold sequence (typically email 4 or 5)
- After a proposal has gone dark for 2+ weeks
- When you've had no engagement across multiple touchpoints

## Key Elements

- **Explicit closure** — "I'm going to stop reaching out" is the whole mechanism
- **No hard feelings** — keep it warm, not bitter or passive-aggressive
- **Easy to reply** — make it effortless for them to say "actually, wait"
- **Brevity** — 3–5 lines max; this is not the place for one more pitch

## Best Practices

- Be genuine about closing the loop — don't fake it
- Leave the door open ("if timing changes") without expectation
- Some reps add a P.S. with one final value hook — optional, use sparingly
- Send mid-week, mid-morning — same logic as cold outreach timing

## Common Mistakes

- Writing a breakup email that's actually just another pitch
- Guilt-tripping ("I've tried reaching you 5 times...")
- Sounding passive-aggressive ("I'll assume you're not interested")
- Going too long — destroys the effect
- Using the breakup email too early in the sequence

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Re: [original thread] — closing the loop

Hi [Name],

I've reached out a few times and haven't heard back — I'll
assume the timing's not right and won't bother you again.

If things change, you know where to find me.

[Name]
```

**Longer variant with a soft hook:**
```
Subject: Closing the loop

Hi [Name],

I've tried a few times without luck — going to stop here and
assume it's not the right moment.

If [pain point or goal] ever comes back to the top of the list,
happy to pick this up. Otherwise, best of luck with [something
specific to their company/role].

[Name]
```

**After a gone-dark proposal:**
```
Subject: Re: [Proposal] — should I close this out?

Hi [Name],

It's been [X weeks] since I sent the proposal and I haven't
heard back. I don't want to keep nudging you — should I
close this out on my end?

If the timing's shifted or something's changed, just say the
word and we can revisit.

[Name]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Reply rate** — breakup emails often hit 10–20% reply rate
- **Recovered deals** — how many "wait, actually..." replies convert
