# Team Announcement

Company-wide or team-wide announcement for significant news: org changes, new hires, promotions, strategy shifts, policy changes, or major milestones.

## When to Send
- Org changes or restructuring: before the rumour mill does it for you
- New hires and promotions: day of or day before their start/effective date
- Policy changes: minimum 1 week lead time for non-urgent changes
- Major milestones: same day as the milestone where possible

## Who Sends It
- **CEO or founder** for company-wide strategic or cultural news
- **Functional lead / VP** for team-level org or role changes
- **People/HR** for policy or process changes

## Best Practices
- Lead with the news in sentence 1 — don't bury the lede with context or backstory
- For org changes: name who is affected, what changes, and when — vague announcements create anxiety
- For new hires: include name, role, start date, team, brief background, and Slack handle — give people enough to actually say hello
- For promotions: state the new title and a brief reason — it signals what the company values
- One page maximum — link to a FAQ or separate doc if more detail is needed

## Common Mistakes
- Announcing changes to the team before the affected person has been told
- No "what does this mean for me?" for changes that affect daily work

## Typical Structure
```
Subject: [What's happening] — [effective date or "effective today"]

Hi [Team / All],

[One-sentence statement of the news.]

[2–3 sentences of context: why this is happening, what it means]

What this means for you:
• [Practical implication 1]
• [Who to contact with questions]

[For new hire:]
Please welcome [Name] as our new [Role], starting [date].
[1–2 sentence background.] Reach them on Slack: @[handle]

[If further reading exists: → Full details / FAQ doc]

[Name], [Title]
```

## Metrics to Track
- **Open rate within 24h** — internal announcements should hit 70–90%; lower = wrong channel (try Slack/intranet)
- **Follow-up question volume to sender/HR** — high volume = announcement lacked key detail
- **Manager awareness check** — for major changes: survey managers 24h post-send on team comprehension
