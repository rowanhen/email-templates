# Waitlist & Early Access

Pre-launch engagement: confirms waitlist signup, grants access, and handles beta invites. Builds anticipation and a qualified early-adopter base.

## When to Send

- **Confirmation:** Immediately on waitlist signup
- **Access granted:** When their spot opens up (batch or rolling)
- **Beta invite:** For a subset of the waitlist — exclusive, time-limited

## Key Elements

- **Confirmation:** Acknowledgement, their position or timeline, what to expect
- **Access granted:** The unlock moment — make it feel like a VIP reveal
- **Beta invite:** Exclusivity framing, what they'll be testing, feedback ask

## Best Practices

- Confirmation email sets expectations — be honest about timeline
- "Access granted" is a high-emotion moment — match the energy
- Beta invites should feel exclusive, not like a mass email
- Collect feedback immediately — beta users are your best research source
- Only use scarcity signals ("[X] spots left") if they're real — fake scarcity destroys trust (see seasonal-campaign.md)

## Common Mistakes

- No confirmation email (users assume their signup was lost)
- Vague timeline ("coming soon") — give a range if you can
- Access granted email that's flat and transactional — this is your product's first impression
- Forgetting to suppress waitlist emails once access is granted

## Compliance

- Waitlist signup = consent to receive waitlist-related emails only, not marketing. Keep a clear legal basis (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) or (b)) and include an unsubscribe option in every email.
- If using a referral-to-move-up mechanic, ensure ePrivacy compliance for communications sent to referred contacts.

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: You're on the list — here's what happens next

Hi [Name], you're in. We'll notify you when [Product] opens up.
Estimated access: [timeframe or "rolling invites"]

[CTA: "Want to move up? Share your link →"]

-- ACCESS GRANTED --
Subject: Your access is ready, [Name] 🚀

[Product] is yours. Get started:
1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]

[CTA: "Activate your account →"]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Waitlist signups** — size and growth rate
- **Confirmation open rate** — baseline for list quality
- **Access-to-activation rate** — % who activate after being granted access
- **Beta feedback completion rate** — % of beta users who submit feedback
