# Referral Program Lifecycle

The full referral journey: invite sent, reward earned, referral converted. Unlike a simple referral ask, this is a structured program with triggers at each stage.

## When to Send

- **Invite email:** 30–60 days post-signup, once the user has seen value
- **Reward earned:** Immediately when a referral signs up or completes a key action
- **Referral converted:** When the referred user converts to paid

## Key Elements

- **Invite:** Clear mechanics (what you give, what they get), shareable link
- **Reward earned:** Celebration, what the reward is, when they'll receive it
- **Converted:** Thank the referrer, confirm the reward, encourage another

## Best Practices

- Double-sided rewards (both parties benefit) outperform one-sided significantly
- Make the sharing mechanic obvious — personal link > generic promo code
- Reward immediately on trigger event, not on a manual review cycle
- Keep program mechanics simple — complexity kills participation

## Common Mistakes

- Burying the reward value — lead with it
- Reward too small to be worth the social capital of a personal recommendation
- No follow-up when a referral signs up (missed engagement moment)
- Missing FTC disclosure — incentivised referrals require clear disclosure that the referrer receives a reward (FTC Endorsement Guides, 16 CFR Part 255)

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Give [X], get [Y] — your referral link inside

Hi [Name], you've been using [Product] for [X days].
Share your link and you both get [reward].

Your link: [unique link]

Disclosure: You'll receive [reward] for each referral who signs up.

[Share now →]
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Participation rate** — % of users who share their link at least once
- **Referral conversion rate** — referred signups who become paying customers
- **CAC via referral** — compare to paid acquisition cost
- **K-factor** — average referrals per referring user × conversion rate of those referrals
