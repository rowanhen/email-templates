# Email Verification

Confirms the user owns the email address they signed up with. A functional email, but still an opportunity to reinforce your brand.

## When to Send

- Immediately on signup (before or alongside the welcome email)
- On email address change

## Key Elements

- **Clear subject line** — "Verify your email" or "Confirm your email address"
- **Verification link/button** — prominent, impossible to miss
- **Expiry notice** — how long the link is valid
- **Fallback** — what to do if the button doesn't work (copy-paste link)
- **Security note** — "If you didn't sign up, ignore this email"

## Best Practices

- Link should expire (24-48 hours is standard)
- Include a plain-text version of the verification URL
- Don't require verification to access the product (let them explore first)
- Make the CTA button large and obvious
- Keep the email extremely short — this is functional, not marketing

## Common Mistakes

- Verification link buried in a wall of text
- No expiry on the link (security risk)
- Blocking all access until verified (high drop-off)
- Not including the plain-text URL fallback
- Sending from a noreply address with no way to get help

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Verify your email address

Hi [Name],

Please confirm your email address to get started:

[Verify Email Button]

Or copy and paste this link: [URL]

This link expires in 24 hours.

Didn't sign up? You can safely ignore this email.
```
