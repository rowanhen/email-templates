# Trial Expiring

Sent when a free trial is ending soon. Goal: convert to paid before access lapses.

## When to Send
- 3 days before expiry (primary send — highest conversion)
- 1 day before expiry (secondary send, if not yet converted)
- 7 days before for trials 14+ days long

## Key Elements
- **Exact expiry date and time** — not "your trial is ending soon"
- **Usage data** — what they've built or done during the trial (personalisation)
- **What happens at expiry** — paused, read-only, deleted? State it clearly
- **Single CTA** — upgrade; nothing competing
- **Objection handling** — pricing FAQ link in footer

## Best Practices

- The 3-day email converts better than the day-before one — lead with it
- If card on file: clearly state they'll be charged on [date] and link to cancel
- Don't use trial expiry as a surprise cancellation — users should know exactly what happens
- Offer a short extension for engaged users who haven't converted (feature-gate)

## Legal / Compliance Notes

- If card collected at trial start: FTC and UK CRA require notice before the first charge
- EU/UK auto-renewal: explicit reminder required before trial converts to paid subscription
- Must include a cancel or "don't charge me" link if a card is on file

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: Your [Product] trial ends [in 3 days / tomorrow] — [date]

Hi [Name],

Your free trial of [Product] expires on [date] at [time timezone].

[Since you joined, you've [created X / connected Y / done Z].]

After [date]: [access paused / read-only / data retained for X days]

[Start [Plan Name] — [price]/month →]

[See what's included] · [Pricing FAQ]

[If card on file:]
We'll charge [amount] on [date] unless you cancel: [cancel link]

[No card yet:]
Add a payment method to continue: [link]

[Name], [Company]
```

## Metrics to Track
- **Trial-to-paid conversion** — benchmark: 15-25% for PLG products
- **Conversion by email vs. in-app** — tells you which channel drives upgrades
- **Day-of-expiry cancellations** — high rate = trial didn't deliver enough value
