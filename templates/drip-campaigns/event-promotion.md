# Event Promotion Sequence

Multi-touch sequence for webinars, conferences, and product launches. Maximise registrations, then show-up rate, then post-event conversion.

## When to Use
- Webinars, live demos, virtual summits, in-person events, product launches
- Adapt timing to scale: 3 emails for a weekly webinar; 6–8 for a major launch
- Send to warm lists — cold outbound event promotion needs its own playbook

## Sequence Structure

| Email | Timing | Goal |
|-------|--------|------|
| 1 | 2–3 weeks out | Announce; drive initial registrations |
| 2 | 1 week out | Value reminder + social proof ("X registered") |
| 3 | 1 day before | Logistics + anticipation build |
| 4 | Day of (morning) | "It's today" — include join link prominently |
| 5 | 24h post-event | Replay/recap to non-attendees; extend the offer |
| 6 | 3–5 days post | Follow-up CTA for non-converting registrants |

## Best Practices
- Email 1 sells the *outcome* of attending, not the agenda — "You'll leave knowing how to [X]" beats "Join us as we discuss [topic]"
- Email 3 and 4: include one-click calendar links (Google, Apple, Outlook) — directly increases show-up rate
- Post-event email (5) often outperforms registration emails for conversion — replay + limited-time offer is the proven pattern
- Segment after email 1: registered vs. non-registered get different messaging from email 2 onward

## Typical Structure
```
[Announcement:]
Subject: [Webinar]: How to [outcome] — [Date], [Time TZ]

Hi [Name],

[One-line hook: the problem this event solves]

Join us [Day, Date] at [Time TZ] for [Event Name].

You'll walk away knowing:
• [Specific outcome 1]
• [Specific outcome 2]

[→ Register free — 30 seconds]

---
[Day-before reminder:]
Subject: Tomorrow: [Event] — your join link

📅 [Google] · [Apple] · [Outlook]
🔗 Join: [URL]
```

## Metrics to Track
- **Registration-to-attendee rate** — benchmark 30–50%; below 20% = fix reminder cadence
- **Post-event conversion rate** — % of attendees who take the promoted action within 7 days
- **Replay engagement** — views and CTA clicks from email 5 (often highest-intent cohort)
