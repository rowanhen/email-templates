# Meeting Request Email

Asking for a call, demo, or intro meeting. The goal is to reduce friction to yes — make it as easy as possible to say "sure, when?"

## When to Send

- After a prospect has shown interest (replied, clicked, engaged on LinkedIn)
- After a warm intro or referral
- As a direct outreach when you already have strong context

## Key Elements

- **Clear purpose** — what will they get out of 15/30 minutes?
- **Specific time options** — don't make them think about scheduling
- **Duration commitment** — "15 minutes" outperforms "30 minutes" or "a quick call"
- **Scheduling link** — Calendly/Cal.com reduces back-and-forth; include it but also offer specific times

## Best Practices

- Name the outcome, not the meeting ("show you how X works" > "have a call")
- Offer 2–3 specific windows, then the link — choice without overwhelm
- Match the ask to the relationship stage (15 min for cold, 30 min for warm)
- Confirm the prospect's timezone if you're not sure

## Common Mistakes

- "Let me know what works for you" — puts all the work on them
- Asking for an hour first contact — too big an ask
- Vague agenda ("just to chat" / "learn more about each other")
- Sending calendar link only without any context
- Requesting a specific date far out without alternatives

## Typical Structure

```
Subject: 15 min to show you [specific outcome]?

Hi [Name],

[1-sentence context — why you're reaching out or why now.]

I'd love to show you [specific thing: "how we automated X for Y-type
companies"] — should take 15 minutes.

Are you free for any of these?
- [Day], [Date] at [Time] [TZ]
- [Day], [Date] at [Time] [TZ]
- [Day], [Date] at [Time] [TZ]

Or grab a slot here: [Calendly link]

[Name]
```

**Example:**
```
Subject: 15 min to show you the reconciliation workflow?

Hi Marcus,

You mentioned your team spends ~3 hours/week reconciling invoices
manually — that's the exact thing we built this for.

Would love to walk you through it. Are you free for any of these?
- Tuesday 14th at 10 AM GMT
- Wednesday 15th at 2 PM GMT
- Thursday 16th at 9 AM GMT

Or grab any slot: calendly.com/example

Dan
```

## Metrics to Track

- **Meeting acceptance rate** — replies that convert to booked meetings
- **Show rate** — prospects who actually show up vs. ghost
- **Time-to-book** — how many hours/days between email and booked slot
